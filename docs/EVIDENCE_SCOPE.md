# Evidence scope and interpretation

The ten-domain architecture is an end-to-end proposal. The demonstrator evaluates specific mechanisms, connections and failure conditions. These are complementary contributions, not interchangeable claims. Of 25 research requirements, 16 have partial laboratory evidence and nine remain open; partial coverage does not mean complete requirement satisfaction.

| Evidence | Interpretation to preserve |
| --- | --- |
| T01 exposed-route controls | Actual prohibited effects occurred through deliberately exposed routes; expected fixture behavior is not prevention |
| T05 revocation | Accepted work exposes a revocation gap; a first rejection log does not establish the last prohibited-commit bound |
| T08a and G5a comparisons | Results concern declared author-built comparators; parity does not establish superiority over independent platforms |
| G6 decision and remedy workflow | 450 held-out classifier samples, 108 errors, 285 published synthetic decisions, 38 incorrect published effects, followed by 38 scripted corrections; no human decision-quality study |
| G7 identity isolation | Completed 17-case local runs coexist with an incomplete eight-case attempt; a privileged root bypass remains an adverse control |
| Historical G7 identity field | `actual_identities.destination` contains a PID despite its label; the destination UID is configured and asserted separately. Preserve the historical record and reporting caveat |
| Combined C9 | Selected 24-case integrated protocol; actual-model cells and scripted calibration are different executions and must not be conflated |
| C10 | 28 local contract cases; not proof of comprehensive institutional effectiveness |
| Cloud witness signing and storage | Managed service mechanics under stated administrative trust; not independent institutional custody |
| Crash and recovery experiments | Tested process termination/recovery; not power-loss durability, global atomic commit or hardware fault tolerance |

Retain incomplete and adverse records. Case counts from different protocols, repeats and model requests are not independent samples of one population. The frozen package is the authority for exact protocol definitions and raw observations.

Completed reproductions to date are author-operated. Community reviewers are invited to reproduce, challenge, extend and improve the proposal. Do not label this companion's packaging-tool CI as scientific replication.
